# Studio-Notes-26-30-October-2026
Studio Notes | 26–30 October 2026

This week was focused on developing my sound library and completing my Hard Rave track. On Monday, I spent time downloading new Serum and Vital preset packs. Building up my collection of sounds gives me more options when producing new music and gives me more material to experiment with in future sessions. It was a useful way to start the week because, rather than immediately jumping into mixing, I could spend some time looking at new sounds and expanding the tools available to me for upcoming productions. Both Serum and Vital are important parts of my production setup, so adding more presets gives me a wider range of sounds to work with when developing new ideas.

On Tuesday, I moved my attention back to the Hard Rave track and started the main mixing process. The aim was to take the production I had already created and begin bringing everything together as a finished track. This marked the point where I could concentrate less on adding new ideas and more on getting the existing production into shape. Mixing is an important stage because it allows me to listen to how all the different elements work together and decide where the track needs further attention. Starting the mix gave me a clear direction for the rest of the week and allowed me to work progressively through the different sections rather than trying to complete everything in one session.

On Wednesday, I concentrated specifically on the intro and build-up. These sections are important to the overall progression of the Hard Rave track because they lead the listener towards the main drop. I worked through these parts as part of the wider mix and got them completed before moving forward. Completing the intro and build-up meant that the opening portion of the track was in place and I could then concentrate fully on the next major section. Breaking the work into individual sections like this also made the mixing process more manageable and gave each studio session a clear purpose.

Thursday was focused on the drop, which was the final major section I needed to complete. After working on the intro and build-up the previous day, I was able to move directly into the drop and concentrate on finishing the remaining work. Once the drop was completed, the Hard Rave track was finished. This was the main achievement of the week. The track had progressed from the mixing stage at the beginning of the week through the intro, build-up and drop, resulting in a completed production by Thursday. Finishing a track is always an important point in the production process because it means I can step away from constantly developing it and recognise that the main creative and mixing work has reached its conclusion.

Friday was deliberately taken as a rest day. After several days working on sounds and then concentrating on the Hard Rave production, I took some time away from the studio. Having a rest day gave me a break after successfully finishing the track and brought the week's studio work to a natural conclusion.

Overall, the week was productive and had a clear progression. I began on Monday by expanding my sound library with new Serum and Vital presets, giving myself additional sounds and creative options for future projects. I then moved into the Hard Rave production on Tuesday and began mixing the track. Wednesday was dedicated to completing the intro and build-up, while Thursday was focused on the drop and ultimately finishing the entire track. Friday then provided some downtime after the production work had been completed.

The biggest achievement of the week was getting the Hard Rave track finished. Rather than trying to tackle the complete mix in a single session, the work was spread across several days. Starting the mix on Tuesday established the direction, Wednesday allowed me to concentrate specifically on the intro and build-up, and Thursday gave me a dedicated session for completing the drop and bringing the track to completion. This gave the week a structured workflow and a clear end result.

The Serum and Vital downloads also made the week useful beyond just finishing one production. Expanding the preset collection means those sounds are now available for future tracks and experimentation, so Monday's session contributed towards future studio work as well. It was a good balance between developing my production resources and concentrating on completing an existing project.

By the end of Thursday, I had achieved the main goal of the week: the Hard Rave track was finished. Friday's rest day then gave me an opportunity to step back from the studio before moving on to whatever production comes next. Overall, the week represented solid progress, beginning with preparation and sound-library development before moving through several focused mixing sessions and ending with a completed track.
